Gesture map

Home sits in a horizontal pager: swipe left → Overview, swipe left again → History. Swipe right goes back. Pull down from the top edge → Quick settings from any screen. Swipe up from the bottom edge → back to Home from anywhere. Long press a tile → that medicine's detail page; swipe right from the left edge closes it. The page dots at the bottom show the position. Only scrolling inside the grid uses plain vertical swipes, so the edge gestures never clash with it.

6 · Dose due (full screen)

Chime plays. Slide right = taken (no accidental taps). Swipe left = snooze. Skip is a small text button. With several doses due, dots on the left edge show the position and "+N more" follows the due time. A "missed" event is written if nothing happens within the set time. On USB power the card keeps the screen on, shown here with the bolt.

7 · Logged (single tap on a tile)

The ring resets, the content dims under a scrim and a toast offers Undo for 5 s with a countdown. Shaking the device also undoes and shows "Undone by shake". The top bar stays bright.

8 · Idle / dimmed

After 30 s: dim grey clock and next dose, with the battery percent and Wi-Fi state below. The group moves a few pixels each minute against burn-in. Tap to wake. On battery the screen turns off after a further delay.
